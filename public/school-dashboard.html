Uko ishuri ryoshyiraho abanyeshuri n'ibizamini

Ishuri rishobora kwiyandikisha abanyeshuri bo mu rubuga, rikabashyiraho ibizamini, rikabikoresha mu buryo bworoshye. Abanyeshuri bashobora kwinjira bakarangiza ibizamini byabo uko bishakiye.

1

Andikisha abanyeshuri

Shyiramo amazina y'abanyeshuri kandi ubatange mu portal ya ishuri kugira ngo bagere ku ibizamini.

2

Shyiraho ibizamini

Nyuma yo guhabwa package ya 200 exams, ishuri rishobora kubaha abanyeshuri ibizamini bijyanye n'ubushobozi bwabo.

3

Gukurikirana amanota

Portal itanga amakuru y'ibizamini byakozwe, amanota, n'ibyavuye byoroshye kuri dashboard ya ishuri.

4

Uburyo bworoshye

Abanyeshuri bashobora gukora ibizamini byabo, maze ishuri rikabona umusaruro w'ibizamini ku buryo bwihuse.

By'ingenzi

  • Package ya 200 exams igura 10,000 Frw.
  • Igiciro ku exam ni 50 Frw.
  • Ishuri rishobora gutegeka no gukurikirana ibizamini by'abanyeshuri.
  • Amanota agaragara mu dashboard kugira ngo ayoborwe byoroshye.
