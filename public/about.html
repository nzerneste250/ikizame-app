About IKIZAME

IKIZAME is an online learning platform designed to help learners in Rwanda prepare for their driving license examinations with confidence. Our mission is to make exam preparation simple, affordable, and accessible from anywhere.

We provide practice questions, learning resources, and a clear path for users who want to study at their own pace. Our platform is built to support both individual learners and driving schools that want to offer better preparation tools to their students.

At IKIZAME, we believe that access to quality education should be easy and reliable. That is why we continue to improve our platform to serve learners better every day.
