Company Overview

About IKIZAME

An online learning and preparation platform operated by DOTADO STATIONERY STORE LTD, helping candidates prepare for the official provisional driving license theory examination in Rwanda.

IKIZAME is an independent educational platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Rwanda National Police or any government institution.
Who We Are

DOTADO STATIONERY STORE LTD operates IKIZAME

IKIZAME provides users with access to educational resources and practice tests that help them evaluate their knowledge, improve their understanding of road safety rules, and increase their readiness before attending the official examination provided by the Rwanda National Police.

IKIZAME does not conduct official driving examinations, issue driving licenses, or provide official examination certificates. Our role is purely educational — to prepare you before you sit the real test.

Our Services

What IKIZAME Provides

Online practice tests designed to simulate the official provisional driving license examination format
Access to driving theory practice questions and learning materials
Mock examinations that replicate the experience of a real theory test
Instant results and performance evaluation to identify areas for improvement
Premium access plans providing additional practice tests and educational content
Continuous improvement and updating of learning materials

All services are delivered electronically through the IKIZAME website after successful payment activation.
