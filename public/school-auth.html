School Portal Guide

Follow these steps to create your school account, login, and get verified.

1
Register

Create School Account

Click "Fungura Konti Hano", fill in your school name, email address, and phone number, then submit.

2
Verify

Confirm OTP Code

Check your school email inbox for a 6-digit OTP code and enter it to verify your account.

3
Password

Set Your Password

Create a strong password for your school portal account. You will use it every time you log in.

4
Login

Login to Dashboard

Use your email and password to log in and access your school dashboard to track students.

5
Approval

Get Verified by Admin

IKIZAME admin reviews and activates your account before full access is granted.
