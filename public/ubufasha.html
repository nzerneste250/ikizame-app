Intambwe z'Ikizamini

Kurikiza iyi mbonerahamwe y'intambwe zo gukora ikizamini kuva ku ntangiriro kugeza ku musozo:

1

Kwiyandikisha ku Ntangiriro (Index Page Gate)

Injiza Amazina yanyu neza hamwe na Nomero ya telephone ikoreshwa (itangira na 7). Hano uhitamo niba uri umukandida gitiyeyo cyangwa umunyeshuri w'ishuri rya shofere.

2

Igenzura rya Token (Wallet Access Check)

Sisitemu ihita igenzura niba nomero yawe cyangwa ishuri ryawe rifite inshuro z'ibizamini zihagije. Niba ari ntabwo, unyura ku gice cy'Ibiciro ukagura imfashanyigisho ihita ifunguka muli MoMo PIN Push.

3

Gukora Ikizamini (Live Exam Workspace)

Urupapuro ruhita rurundanya ibibazo 20 byatoranyijwe muli database mu buryo bw'akamashu (shuffled random pool). Usubiza buri kibazo uhitamo imvugo cyangwa ishusho ikwiye muli minutu 20.

4

Gusubiza & Guhabwa Amanota (Exam End & Score)

Kanda "Submit" ubashe gisoza. Inshuro 1 ihita igabanyuka kuri konti yawe, hanyuma sisitemu ikwereke amanota wagize muli Sec 2, ndetse n'ibisubizo byose birambuye (Historical Review Panel).
